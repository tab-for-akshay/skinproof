4 · Is it working? Trials

A trial tracks the concerns a product targets. Re-scan every two weeks in the same light. SkinProof only calls a change real if it is bigger than the measurement noise (shown as a band) and holds for two check-ins. Untargeted control concerns should stay flat, and redness is always watched for irritation.

The two demo trials use synthetic faces: Priya's check-ins were made with YouCam Skin Simulation, Maya's with an image filter that adds redness. Every score shown is a real YouCam Skin Analysis measurement of those images. Your own trials stay on this device.

How it works

YouCam APIs used

  • AI Skin Analysis (SD, 15 concerns + skin type, with mask overlays) for every scan and check-in.
  • AI Skin Simulation to show a realistic, moderate improvement target for the concerns a product actually targets.
  • JS Camera Kit for guided capture (face position, lighting, angle) — fewer bad photos, steadier scores.
  • AI Image Generator V2 to create the synthetic sample faces, so the demo uses no real person's face.
  • Unit System API to protect the demo budget (live scans pause before units run out; the remaining units are shown at the top).

Agent workflow

  1. Measure (Skin Analysis) → pick your 3 weakest concerns.
  2. Ground: parse the INCI list and match a sourced knowledge base (no free-text ingredient claims).
  3. Score fit (benefit vs. cautions for your skin) in code.
  4. Explain: an open AI model writes the plain-language verdict from those facts only; a code guard discards any draft that names an ingredient the product doesn't contain, and the facts are always shown beside it.
  5. Visualise the target (Skin Simulation), then track the trial and give a verdict that respects measurement noise.

We measured the noise

Three faces, each re-analysed after +10% brightness with JPEG recompression and after a 3° tilt with a shifted crop (6 re-captures per concern). Largest score change:

SkinProof uses a band of max(3, largest + 1) points. Real life is noisier (other light, distance, phone), so this band is a floor: SkinProof also shows a capture checklist, requires two check-ins in a row, and labels each verdict's confidence.

Not medical advice. SkinProof is a consumer measurement and shopping aid; for pain, cysts or a spreading rash, see a dermatologist.
