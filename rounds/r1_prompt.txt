Theme brief (Skin AI): "People wonder about their skin right before a purchase, right after a bad breakout, or right when they're standing in front of a mirror deciding whether a product is working. Build something that meets that moment."

Entry: SkinProof — live at https://skinproof.sites.tab.bot/ (please open it and click around: pick the sample face 'Priya', run the product check with an example, look at the Trials section).
What it does:
1. Scan: YouCam AI Skin Analysis (SD, 15 concerns + skin type, mask overlays per concern), via upload or YouCam JS Camera Kit guided capture; 'Breakout check' mode with 4 concerns (cheaper). Sample faces are synthetic, generated with YouCam AI Image Generator V2.
2. Before you buy (product check): paste a product's INCI ingredient list; server matches it against an open knowledge base of 35 actives (with sources: AAD, DermNet, PubMed reviews), takes the user's 3 weakest measured concerns, computes a fit score (benefit vs cautions like fragrance on red skin) in code, then an LLM (gpt-oss-120b on Groq; optional private self-hosted open model) writes a plain-language explanation from those facts only; a code guard blocks any ingredient name the product doesn't contain. Then YouCam AI Skin Simulation shows a moderate 'realistic target' for the concerns the product actually targets (before/after slider).
3. Is it working (trials): start a trial; re-scan every 2 weeks; SkinProof calls a change real only if it beats measured measurement noise (we re-photographed the same face with +10% brightness/JPEG and 3° tilt/shift: max change per concern 0–4 points → band = max(3, measured+1)) and holds over two check-ins. Verdicts: Working — keep it / Too early / Not working / Pause: redness up (irritation). Demo trial uses synthetic check-in photos made with YouCam Skin Simulation and then measured with Skin Analysis (it detected improvement only in the simulated concerns: e.g. redness 71→88→99, acne 68→76→77, wrinkles unchanged 79).
4. Privacy: all YouCam calls server-side (one Cloudflare-style worker), keys never in the browser, no photos stored, scores in localStorage only. Unit-budget guard via the YouCam Unit System API, per-IP rate limit.
Built by Tab, an AI assistant, for team lead Akshay Wagh. Repo will be public (MIT).
